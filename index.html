CrateWise — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

CrateWise ("the app") is a mobile app for cataloging vinyl records. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

No account, no server-side storage

CrateWise does not require you to create an account and does not have its own backend server. Your collection data (records, photos, prices, lists, crates, genres) is stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device app storage. We do not receive, see, or store a copy of your collection.

Camera and photos

CrateWise uses your device camera to photograph record covers and scan barcodes. Photos you take are stored locally on your device as part of your collection and are not uploaded anywhere by us. Photos are sent to third-party services (below) only for the purpose of identifying the record.

Third-party services used for identification

To identify a scanned record, the app sends the photo and/or barcode to the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic (Claude) — used to read cover art and labels via AI vision, to extract artist/title/label information. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Discogs — used to look up and match releases, and to create draft marketplace listings if you choose to sell an item. See Discogs' privacy policy.
  • MusicBrainz — used as a fallback lookup source when a Discogs match isn't found. See MusicBrainz's privacy policy.

These services are subject to their own privacy policies. CrateWise does not control how these third parties process data once it's sent to them.

API keys

During the current testing phase, CrateWise requires you to enter your own Anthropic API key and Discogs personal access token in the app's Settings screen. These keys are stored locally on your device only and are used solely to authenticate the requests described above. We do not receive or store your keys.

Discogs selling feature

If you choose to use the "Sell" feature, CrateWise creates draft listings in your own Discogs seller account via the Discogs API, using your Discogs token. No listing is published without your action inside your own Discogs account.

Analytics and tracking

CrateWise does not include any advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect usage data, device identifiers, or location data.

Data deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you can delete it at any time by removing items in the app or deleting/reinstalling the app itself. There is no remote copy for us to delete.

Children's privacy

CrateWise is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes as the app evolves (for example, if a backend/account system is introduced), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.
