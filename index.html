CrateWise — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

CrateWise ("the app") is a mobile app for cataloging vinyl records. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Account and server-side storage

CrateWise requires an account, created via Google, Apple, or email sign-in. Once signed in, your collection data (artist, title, label, catalog number, year, format, genre, condition, notes, crate/bin location, cover art URL, price, and the lists/crates you organize records into) is stored on our server (hosted on Supabase) so it syncs across your devices. This data is access-controlled so that only your own account can read or write it.

Camera and photos

CrateWise uses your device camera to photograph record covers and scan barcodes. Photos you take are stored locally on your device as part of your collection. When you scan a record, the photo (and/or barcode) is sent to third-party services (below) to identify it. We do not upload or store a copy of your original photos on our server — only the identified record's metadata (artist, title, etc., described above) is saved server-side.

Third-party services used for identification

To identify a scanned record, the app sends the photo and/or barcode — via our own server — to the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic (Claude) — used to read cover art and labels via AI vision, to extract artist/title/label information. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Discogs — used to look up and match releases. See Discogs' privacy policy.
  • MusicBrainz — used as a fallback lookup source when a Discogs match isn't found. See MusicBrainz's privacy policy.

These services are subject to their own privacy policies. CrateWise does not control how these third parties process data once it's sent to them. To speed up repeat scans, we also maintain a shared, de-identified cache of previously-identified releases (metadata only, not your photo, not linked to your account) that any user's scan can match against.

Analytics and tracking

CrateWise does not include any advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect usage data, device identifiers, or location data.

Data deletion

Photos and any locally-cached view of your collection can be removed at any time by deleting items in the app or deleting/reinstalling the app itself. To delete your account and all associated server-side data (collection and lists), email topdonn1@gmail.com from the address associated with your account — we'll confirm once it's done. A self-serve in-app delete option is planned but not yet available.

Children's privacy

CrateWise is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes as the app evolves, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.
